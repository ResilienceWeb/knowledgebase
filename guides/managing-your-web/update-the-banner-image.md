# Update the banner image

The banner image (also called the cover image) is the picture that represents your web on the [resilienceweb.org.uk](https://resilienceweb.org.uk) homepage. Choose a photo that represents your web well — maybe the skyline of your town, or a local nature spot. Please use a photo that you have taken or own the rights to.

To update it:

1. Sign in to the admin dashboard and go to **Web Settings** (only web owners can access this page).
2. Upload or replace the picture in the **Image** section.
3. Press **Update** to save your changes.
