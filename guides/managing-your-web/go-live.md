# Go live

Publishing a web is currently a manual process — we review each web before it goes live. When your web is ready to share with the world, please email [info@resilienceweb.org.uk](mailto:info@resilienceweb.org.uk) and we'll review and publish it for you.

Before getting in touch, please make sure that:

* your web has at least 10 listings with complete information and images
* your web has a cover image uploaded in Web Settings
* the contact email field in Web Settings is filled out

Until your web is published, it is only visible to people who have the link, so you can experiment freely while you build it up.

Once your web goes live, it will be listed on the main page at [resilienceweb.org.uk](https://resilienceweb.org.uk), and we will also advertise it to our mailing list and on social media.
